Datatin

Datatin is an independent YouTube channel that turns open public statistics (Eurostat, NASA, national statistics offices) into animated chart videos: a daily Short and two longer videos a week. Every video cites its source.

Datatin Uploader

Datatin Uploader is the private tool the channel owner uses to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube API Services, only after the owner has reviewed and approved each video. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the channel owner.
